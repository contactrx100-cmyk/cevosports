PRIVACY.

Last updated 17 November 2026

WHO WE ARE

Cevosports, Norway, is the data controller for this waitlist. Contact: hello@cevosports.com.

WHAT WE COLLECT

  • Your email address and when you joined.
  • If you choose to tell us: what you're pursuing and your story.
  • If you unsubscribe: the time you did it, so we never email you again.

WHY, AND ON WHAT BASIS

To give you your member number and send you emails about Cevosports: the welcome email, updates and early access when we launch. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)), which you give when you join. Joining is voluntary, but without an email address we can't add you to the waitlist. We never sell your data, share it for marketing or use it for ads, and we make no automated decisions about you.

YOUR DATA IS SAFE

Your email is stored securely with Google, the same protection behind Gmail. Only Cevosports has access.

We use a few trusted providers to run the waitlist. They only process data on our behalf:

  • Google (Gmail, Google Sheets and Google Fonts): stores the list and sends the emails.
  • GitHub and Cloudflare: host the website and domain. Like any website, they may log basic technical data such as your IP address to keep the site running and secure.

These providers may process data in the USA. The transfer is protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the EU's standard contractual clauses.

HOW LONG

Until you unsubscribe, ask us to delete your data, or the waitlist closes. After that it is deleted. If you unsubscribe, we mark you as unsubscribed so you never get more emails. Ask us, and we delete everything.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can at any time ask to see, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to how we use it, or get a copy of it. You can withdraw your consent whenever you want by using the unsubscribe link in any email or writing to hello@cevosports.com. This does not affect what we did before you withdrew it. We answer within 30 days.

If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (datatilsynet.no).
