WHO WE ARE

Cevosports is responsible for the data on this waitlist. Questions or requests: hello@cevosports.com.

WHAT WE COLLECT

  • Your email address and when you joined.
  • If you choose to tell us: what you're pursuing and your story.

WHY

To give you your member number and send you emails about Cevosports: the welcome email, updates and early access when we launch. We do this because you joined and agreed to it. We never sell or share your data.

YOUR DATA IS SAFE

Your email is stored securely with Google, the same protection behind Gmail. Only Cevosports has access, and we never sell it, share it or use it for ads.

The website runs on GitHub and Cloudflare, two of the world's biggest web platforms. Like any website, they may log basic technical data such as your IP address to keep the site running and secure.

These providers may process data outside the EU/EEA, protected by EU-approved safeguards.

HOW LONG

Until you unsubscribe, ask us to delete it, or the waitlist closes.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can see, correct or delete your data at any time. Unsubscribe with the link in any email, or write to hello@cevosports.com. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (datatilsynet.no).
